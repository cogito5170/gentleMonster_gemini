# Gentle Monster — Steam Alley
*A winter sauna, 8 metres wide*

You step in from a cold alley and the air changes: warm wood on every surface, a low light, and a faint mist under the ceiling. In the middle a dark stone stove sits on the slatted floor with a heavy knit draped over it, as if someone left it there to dry. Wooden shelves run down both long walls, stacked with folded sweaters in the colours of a winter forest. At the back a fogged glass screen hides the counter, and you only see its warm glow through the steam.

**Keywords:** Warmth · Steam · Knit
**Philosophy:** Come in from the cold and stay a while.

## Design intent
- **The whole alley is the sauna** Wood covers floor, walls and ceiling of the 8 x 10 m room, so the narrow plan feels like one warm cabin.
- **The stove is the centre** A stone stove with a draped knit stands mid-room; the route loops around it at more than a metre.
- **Knit lines both long walls** Two 6.5 m shelf walls hold the collection, so every step of the loop passes product.
- **Glass keeps the counter in the steam** A fogged glass screen 7.6 m in separates the counter, which glows through it at the end of the loop.

## The walk
- **The cold stops at the door.** A stone stove waits in the mist with a sweater drying on it.
- **The walls are stacked with winter.** Folded knits in spruce, ember and undyed wool.
- **Someone is behind the steam.** The counter glows through fogged glass.
