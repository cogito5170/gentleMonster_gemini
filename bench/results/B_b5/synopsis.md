# Gentle Monster — Orbital Hangar 01
*The zero-gravity docking sequence*

You step into a cavernous metallic hangar where the air hums with low-frequency magnetic resonance. Above you, hundreds of sunglasses float in a state of perpetual freefall, held by invisible currents within a cold, brushed-aluminium chamber. The floor is a dark, matte graphite that absorbs all sound, forcing your movement to become a slow, rhythmic glide toward the center. As you reach for a frame, the surrounding glass walls ripple with soft pulses of white light, simulating the docking approach of a deep-space vessel.

**Keywords:** Weightlessness · Magnetic · Stasis
**Philosophy:** In the silence of the hangar, your gaze is the only anchor.

## Design intent
- **Spatial compression** The narrow entry zone mimics an airlock, forcing the visitor to slow down before the hangar volume expands.
- **Floating focal point** The hero display is positioned at the exact center of the room, requiring the visitor to circulate around it like a satellite.
- **Visual weight** The floating eyewear is arranged in an elliptical array to reinforce the sensation of an orbital trajectory.
- **Material contrast** Graphite floors contrast with the polished aluminium walls to heighten the illusion of a metallic spacecraft interior.

## The walk
- **The hangar opens.** Weightlessness takes hold as the drift begins.
- **Docking sequence initiated.** The light pulses in sync with my approach to the center.
- **Suspended in the void.** The sunglasses float like stars in the hangar.
