# Tamburins — Salt Desert Silence
*An ethereal fragrance sanctuary in the heart of Busan*

You enter a vast, mineral-white clearing that captures the profound stillness of an ancient salt desert. As you walk along the central path of exposed strata clay, the stark void forces you to focus on the subtle shifting of light across the monochromatic terrain. You eventually reach the central salt dune monolith, where the surrounding fragrance clusters reveal themselves only as you draw near. This spatial rhythm transforms the act of shopping into a meditative exploration of scent and solitude.

**Keywords:** Minimalism · Olfactory · Ethereal
**Philosophy:** In the absence of clutter, the scent becomes the only truth.

## Design intent
- **Cracked Earth Threshold** A cracked earth path runs 4.7 m from the door toward the salt dune monolith. This linear path channels your movement from the city bustle into the quiet stillness of the desert floor.
- **Monolithic Focal Point** The salt dune monolith stands alone 5.6 m inside the door, 3.5 m tall; nothing else rises above 1.8 m. The towering central monolith creates a sense of awe while keeping your sightlines clear across the vast, empty space.
- **Proximity-Based Discovery** Four fragrance salt clusters stations hold the product in a loose ring around it, 0.4 m from the route at the closest. These low-profile salt clusters ensure you must pause and lean in to experience the fragrance, deepening your personal connection to the product.
- **Hidden Transaction Point** The minimalist transaction block sits at the back right, where the route ends. By tucking the lime plaster counter at the end of the route, the design ensures the commerce remains secondary to your sensory experience.

## The walk
- **I step onto the cracked strata clay path leading into the void.** The tactile surface grounds my movement as the expansive white space stretches out before me.
- **I stand before the monolith, a towering pillar of salt rising from the dust.** Its textured, mineral surface captures the shifting light, anchoring the entire silent landscape.
- **I lean in to examine the crystalline fragrance clusters nestled in the salt.** These delicate formations release faint, ethereal scents that reward my quiet, focused approach.
