# Gentle Monster — The Threshold of a Rainy Night
*Where silence falls between the drops*

As you step inside, the abrasive roar of the Seoul rain abruptly vanishes, replaced by a hollow, ringing silence. You stand upon dark, slick granite that reflects the dim, oscillating pulses of overhead light beams. Ahead, massive blocks of rough-hewn stone emerge from the gloom like monoliths left in a storm, their surfaces glistening with synthetic moisture. You navigate through these jagged volumes, uncovering eyewear hidden within the crevices as if finding relics washed up on a shore.

**Keywords:** Threshold · Stasis · Submersion
**Philosophy:** The storm ends where your reflection begins.

## Design intent
- **Acoustic transition** The layout channels visitors into a central void immediately after the door, forcing a physical pause that breaks the city's momentum.
- **Materiality of rain** The use of black stone and graphite wax creates a perpetually wet texture that visually translates the rainy theme.
- **Heroic distribution** The placement of monoliths forces a non-linear path, ensuring the visitor is always turning to find products hidden in stone gaps.
- **Lighting as mood** Low-intensity, focused spotlights create high-contrast shadows, mimicking the street lighting of a rainy night.

## The walk
- **The roar of the city fades into silence.** The light catches the wet edges of the dark stone.
- **Relics hidden in the crevices.** Eyewear rests within the cold stone gaps.
- **The end of the threshold.** The transaction marks the exit from the storm.
