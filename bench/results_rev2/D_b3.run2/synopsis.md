# Aesop — The Archive of Scents
*Aesop Jeju: Where literature ends and fragrance begins.*

You step into a space that feels like a forgotten archive, where the walls are composed of dark Jeju stone. The central stone basin serves as the anchor, inviting you to perform a silent, ritualistic cleansing that cleanses the mind as well as the hands. As you follow the path, the warmth of the shelves replaces the cold stone, guiding you through a collection of scents that mirror the depth of a library. Each step here is a journey through memory and scent, sheltered from the world outside.

**Keywords:** archival silence · volcanic whispers · ritualistic cleansing
**Philosophy:** An olfactory library carved from the breath of Jeju.

## Design intent
- **The shelf walls define the boundaries of the** Shelf walls (Shelves for fragrance bottles) wrap the left, right and back of the 12 x 10 m room. The shelf walls define the boundaries of the room, creating an immersive, book-like enclosure for the product.
- **The central stone basin acts as the primary** The centre object (The central stone basin) is the first stop, 4.2 m in from the door. The central stone basin acts as the primary sensory focal point, grounding your visit in a moment of quiet reflection.
- **The circulation encourages a contemplative** The route circles it first, then follows the shelves. The circulation encourages a contemplative pace, moving from the cleansing ritual to the discovery of the scents.
- **The counter at the back provides a final** The counter (Service counter at the back) sits at the back, off the loop. The counter at the back provides a final, grounded point for interaction before you complete your journey.

## The walk
- **I stand before the silent basin, the heart of this stone sanctuary.** The water ripples against the dark, grounding volcanic rock.
- **I graze the wooden shelves, tracing scents like spines of books.** Fragrance replaces ink in this archive of Jeju memories.
- **I pause at the back, feeling the stillness of the library.** A quiet ritual completed in the shadow of the cooling light.
