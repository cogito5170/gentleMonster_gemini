# Gentle Monster — The Rainy Threshold
*Seongsu Flagship Store*

You step past the threshold pillars into a hushed space where the outside storm instantly vanishes. Before you lies the Wet Obsidian Monolith, standing in solitary stillness amid polished concrete. You wander around the empty void, discovering eyewear resting upon minimalist displays at the edges of the room.

**Keywords:** threshold · monolith · silence
**Philosophy:** Silence is carved in stone when the rain stops.

## Design intent
- **They filter the noisy exterior world, preparing** A pair of threshold objects (Basalt Threshold Pillars) stands 1.0-3.0 m inside the door, with a hanging screen (Rain Curtain) between them; the route slips through a 1.9 m gap. They filter the noisy exterior world, preparing you to enter a serene interior.
- **It commands your attention from afar, anchoring** The hero (Wet Obsidian Monolith) stands alone in an empty 8 x 7 m area (Empty Reflection Void); the route circles it and never comes closer than 3.3 m. It commands your attention from afar, anchoring the vast emptiness of the room.
- **They invite you to pause and examine the** Four product stations (Eyewear Stations) sit at the outer corners, outside the loop, so you turn away from the hero to reach them. They invite you to pause and examine the eyewear in intimate corners away from the central void.
- **It completes your circular journey through the** The pay desk (Monolith Pay Desk) closes the loop at the back wall. It completes your circular journey through the dark gallery before you step back out.

## The walk
- **I step inside as the sound of rain immediately cuts off behind me.** Basalt Threshold Pillars
- **I stand at the edge of the vast void, watching the silent monolith.** Wet Obsidian Monolith
- **I turn toward the outer corner to inspect the eyewear collection.** Eyewear Stations
