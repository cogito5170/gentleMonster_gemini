# Gentle Monster — The Dry Pool
*An empty pool, a silent expanse, a new clarity.*

You descend the polished concrete stairs, entering a space where the air feels heavy with history. At the center stands a waterless porcelain basin, its surface catching the beams of refracted light that filter from above. As you navigate the perimeter, you find eyewear resting atop floating glass displays, echoing the quiet stillness of the basin. The journey ends at a weathered desk, leaving you within the pristine geometry of a memory.

**Keywords:** refracted light · echoing silence · suspended time
**Philosophy:** Water is absent, but the light still remembers the depth.

## Design intent
- **The floor path draws you downward into the** A floor strip (descending pool ladder path) runs 4.7 m from the door toward the hero (waterless porcelain basin). The floor path draws you downward into the pool's hollow core.
- **The central basin anchors the expansive, open** The hero stands alone 5.6 m inside the door, 3.5 m tall; nothing else rises above 1.8 m. The central basin anchors the expansive, open room with its silent presence.
- **These displays allow for intimate interaction** Four product stations (floating glass eyewear displays) ring it loosely, 0.4 m from the route at the closest. These displays allow for intimate interaction with the collection while maintaining the pool's stark feel.
- **The desk marks the transition from the dream** The pay counter (sun-bleached payment desk) sits at the back right, where the route ends. The desk marks the transition from the dream back to the reality of the exit.

## The walk
- **I stand at the threshold, the ground sloping downward into the vast, hollow** The descent feels like stepping back into a forgotten season.
- **I walk up to the waterless porcelain basin, tracing the contours where water** The light fractures across its smooth, dry edge, mimicking the ripples of a memory.
- **I lean into the glass display, where the eyewear floats in the suspended** Even in the absence of water, the clarity remains absolute.
