# Aesop — Jeju Scent Library
*A meditative retreat of stone and fragrance.*

You enter a room defined by textured strata clay walls that hold bottles like archived literature. In the absolute centre, a black volcanic stone basin serves as a meditative anchor where you pause to cleanse your hands. As you circle this quiet ritual, the scent of the Jeju earth and the gentle flow of water transport you into a realm of deep stillness. Finally, you move to the warm wooden counter at the back, completing your journey through this sensory archive.

**Keywords:** Library of Scent · Volcanic Serenity · Aromatic Ritual
**Philosophy:** The silence of a library meets the scent of earth and time.

## Design intent
- **Layered Earth Walls** Shelf walls (back library shelf wall) wrap the left, right and back of the 12 x 10 m room. These layered walls envelop you in the tactile essence of the island, creating a grounded, intimate library atmosphere.
- **Meditative Basin Centre** The centre object (central meditative basin) is the first stop, 4.2 m in from the door. This central water feature offers an immediate sensory pause, inviting you to clear your mind and senses upon entry.
- **Guided Ritual Journey** The route circles it first, then follows the shelves. The circulation forces a slow, contemplative pace that mirrors the reverence of an ancient library.
- **Warm Timber Counter** The counter (back service counter) sits at the back, off the loop. This final point of contact provides a grounding conclusion to your aromatic exploration within the space.

## The walk
- **I stand before the volcanic stone basin, the weight of the library air** The sound of water ripples against the dark, porous stone.
- **I brush my hand along the clay wall, the texture whispering stories of earth** Amber bottles are nested perfectly within the sedimentary layers.
- **Looking up towards the ceiling, I feel the sanctuary shift into a quiet, warm** A final moment of stillness before departing the scent archive.
