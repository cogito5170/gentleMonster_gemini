# Tamburins — The Salt Sanctuary
*A fragrance journey through the quiet salt flats of Busan.*

You step into a world of blinding whiteness, where the stillness of a salt desert greets you. Guided by a path of silence, you approach the pristine white salt mound standing as a solitary monument in the center. As you circle this void, you discover hidden perfume vessels nestled within the landscape, waiting to be found. Every breath here is a meditation, a delicate balance between the vastness of the space and the intimacy of the scent.

**Keywords:** silence · minimalism · salt desert
**Philosophy:** In the vast silence of salt, even the smallest essence becomes an infinity.

## Design intent
- **Pathway of absolute stillness** A floor strip (pathway of silence) runs 4.7 m from the door toward the hero (pristine white salt mound). This path draws you into the space, grounding you in the profound stillness of the salt desert.
- **A monumental salt horizon** The hero stands alone 5.6 m inside the door, 3.5 m tall; nothing else rises above 1.8 m. This towering, pristine white salt mound creates a focal point that anchors the vast, empty room.
- **Hidden scents of discovery** Four product stations (subtle hidden perfume vessel) ring it loosely, 0.4 m from the route at the closest. These subtle vessels encourage a tactile exploration of the space, turning discovery into a ritual.
- **Transactional serenity** The pay counter (minimalist transactional block) sits at the back right, where the route ends. The minimalist block offers a quiet, final anchor before you leave the sanctuary of the salt.

## The walk
- **I step onto the path, and the world behind me fades into silence.** The floor strip guides me toward the heart of the desert.
- **Before me, the massive salt mound rises, a quiet testament to stillness.** It feels as if the salt has reclaimed the space, leaving only the memory of fragrance.
- **I reach out, and the vessel reveals itself, a hidden treasure among the grains.** The scent lingers in the air, barely whispered, waiting to be found.
