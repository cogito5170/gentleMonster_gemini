# Gentle Monster — The Drained Pool Gallery
*A 1970s sanctuary reclaimed by light.*

You step down into the raw concrete expanse of a drained 1970s indoor pool, where the familiar echoes of water are replaced by a profound, silent stillness. At the center of the deep, you encounter the fragmented prismatic light source, a basin of brilliance that shatters the dim, cavernous atmosphere. You weave between mineral-white monoliths displaying eyewear, each station catching the spectral reflections cast across the clay floor. The journey concludes at the back, where the polished concrete desk awaits in the soft, glowing fog.

**Keywords:** submerged memory · refracted silence · drained architecture
**Philosophy:** To empty the space is to fill it with vision.

## Design intent
- **Descending the threshold** A floor strip (entryway staircase landing) runs 4.7 m from the door toward the hero (fragmented prismatic light source). The landing invites you to descend, shifting your perception from the street level to the subterranean floor of the pool.
- **A beacon of light** The hero stands alone 5.6 m inside the door, 3.5 m tall; nothing else rises above 1.8 m. This basin acts as the physical embodiment of water, turning light into a tangible presence in the empty room.
- **Floating product monoliths** Four product stations (product display monoliths) ring it loosely, 0.4 m from the route at the closest. These stations create a meditative path that mimics the movement of currents, guiding you naturally toward the product.
- **A grounded conclusion** The pay counter (polished payment desk) sits at the back right, where the route ends. The final point of rest provides a grounding finish to your underwater journey.

## The walk
- **I step down, leaving the surface world behind as the concrete floor shifts** The entrance marks the threshold to the deep.
- **I stand before the glowing basin, where light ripples against the walls like** A fluid presence where water used to be.
- **I lean into the display, finding products nestled within the quiet architecture** A curated stillness amidst the vacancy.
