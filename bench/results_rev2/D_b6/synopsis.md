# Gentle Monster — The Sauna's Breath
*A ritual of warmth in a frozen, hidden alley.*

You step off the freezing street into an atmosphere heavy with the scent of pine and gentle steam. At the center of the narrow room, you find the steaming basin of ice, where liquid warmth rises in defiance of the frost. You circle this focal point, your fingers tracing the soft textures displayed on the surrounding plaster shelves, before finally reaching the wooden counter at the back to conclude your journey.

**Keywords:** winter sauna · woolen warmth · steaming silence
**Philosophy:** To wear the knit is to wrap oneself in the quiet memory of a frozen forest.

## Design intent
- **The plaster shelves envelop you, providing a** Shelf walls (knitwear display shelves) wrap the left, right and back of the 8 x 10 m room. The plaster shelves envelop you, providing a tactile experience of the winter-inspired knitwear collection.
- **The steaming basin of ice acts as a sensory** The centre object (steaming basin of ice) is the first stop, 4.2 m in from the door. The steaming basin of ice acts as a sensory anchor, pulling you into the center of the space.
- **The circular flow encourages a meditative pace** The route circles it first, then follows the shelves. The circular flow encourages a meditative pace, mimicking the quiet rhythm of a sauna.
- **The counter offers a grounding, intimate finale** The counter (check-in counter) sits at the back, off the loop. The counter offers a grounding, intimate finale to your ritual of warmth.

## The walk
- **I step into the warmth, my eyes caught by the ethereal shimmer of the amber** The air is thick with a gentle, inviting steam.
- **I reach out to touch the soft knits resting against the textured plaster walls.** The rough material contrasts beautifully with the delicate wool.
- **I stand beneath the warm ceiling light, feeling the sanctuary of this hidden** A perfect moment of stillness before returning to the winter cold.
