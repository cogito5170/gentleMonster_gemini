# Gentle Monster — Threshold of the Rainy Night
*An olfactory sanctuary carved from basalt and reflective horizons.*

As you cross the threshold, the cacophony of the city dissolves into a rhythmic stillness defined by damp, polished concrete floors. You navigate between looming black basalt monoliths that display fragrance vials like sacred artifacts caught in the moonlight. Reflective aluminum walls expand the space, blurring your reflection against the deep, wet textures of the room. By interacting with the crushing stone protrusions, you feel the raw tension between the natural landscape and the refined architectural interior.

**Keywords:** Olfactory Sanctuary · Basalt Minimalism · Atmospheric Threshold
**Philosophy:** Silence is the architecture of rain, and scent is the memory of the stone it touches.

## Design intent
- **The Reflective Horizon** A walled chamber (reflective horizon), 11 x 8 m, stands inside the room; you enter it through a 1.8 m opening in its side. The walled chamber isolates your senses from the external world to create an immersive, meditative environment.
- **Elevated Basalt Monoliths** Three low monoliths (basalt monoliths) inside it carry the product at 0.9-1.4 m high. These stone pedestals force you to slow your pace and engage intimately with the fragrance collection at eye level.
- **Crushing Stone Intersections** Two blocks (crushing stone) break through the chamber walls on opposite sides. The protruding rocks disrupt the perfect geometry of the walls to remind you of the untamed nature breaking through the sterile design.
- **External Functional Zones** The pay desk (dark payment plinth) and the fitting room (soft light chamber) stay outside the chamber. Placing the payment and fitting areas outside the core ensures that the sanctuary remains a place of pure, undisturbed sensory contemplation.

## The walk
- **I trace the jagged, raw edge of the basalt as it ruptures the smooth facade.** The stone feels cold and ancient, a violent intrusion into the store's clinical stillness.
- **I stand before the infinite horizon created by the polished aluminum panels.** My own reflection dissolves into the shimmering depth, merging with the shadows of the room.
- **I reach out to touch the obsidian surface of the central display monolith.** The fragrance vials rest like relics upon the dark stone, illuminated by a faint, lunar glow.
