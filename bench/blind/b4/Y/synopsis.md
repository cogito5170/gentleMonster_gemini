# Tamburins — Tamburins Busan: Salt Horizon
*An ethereal landscape where scent emerges from the silence of salt*

As you step onto the pristine salt floor, the world outside fades into the absolute stillness of a blinding white desert. You follow a singular pathway that leads you toward a towering monolith, feeling the crunch of mineral grains beneath your feet. Only as you approach the scattered scent stones do the hidden fragrances reveal themselves, transforming the empty air into a sensory discovery. You conclude your journey at a quiet plaster counter, where the raw materiality of the room leaves you with a lingering, ethereal memory.

**Keywords:** Minimalist · Olfactory · Ethereal
**Philosophy:** True discovery requires the silence of space and the patience of the senses.

## Design intent
- **Entrance Pathway** A floor strip (salt pathway) runs 4.7 m from the door toward the hero (central salt monolith). This 4.7-meter salt strip establishes a meditative transition that strips away the noise of the city before you reach the fragrance zone.
- **Central Monolith** The hero stands alone 5.6 m inside the door, 3.5 m tall; nothing else rises above 1.8 m. The 3.5-meter central monolith acts as a visual anchor that forces you to navigate around it, keeping the rest of the store feeling vast and empty.
- **Scent Stations** Four product stations (fragrance scent stone) ring it loosely, 0.4 m from the route at the closest. The placement of these stones near the path encourages you to stop and lean in, turning a simple walk into an intimate olfactory encounter.
- **Terminal Counter** The pay counter (minimalist pay counter) sits at the back right, where the route ends. Positioning the minimalist pay counter at the end of the route ensures that your final transaction feels like a natural conclusion to your exploration.

## The walk
- **I step onto the white salt floor and feel the crunch beneath my feet.** A singular pathway guides me forward, stripping away the noise of the outside world.
- **I stand before the towering monolith rising from the desert floor.** Its raw, imposing form anchors the space, creating a sense of absolute stillness.
- **I lean in close to discover the fragrance hidden within the scent stone.** Mineral grains meet delicate notes, turning this quiet corner into a sensory revelation.
