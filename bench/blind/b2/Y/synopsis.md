# Gentle Monster — Submerged Luminescence
*A dry 1970s pool basin where light ripples in place of water.*

You descend into the void, leaving the world behind as you pass between polished concrete pillars and a shimmering aluminum veil. The drained basin centers the space, drawing your gaze toward its brass floor as light filters through a textile ceiling to mimic the movement of water. As you circle the deep end, you uncover sunken glass cases tucked into the corners, inviting you to pause and inspect the collection. Finally, you reach the attendant station, completing your journey through this ethereal, architectural memory.

**Keywords:** caustic light · submerged · nostalgic
**Philosophy:** We replace the weight of water with the fluidity of light to redefine the act of observation.

## Design intent
- **Threshold Transition** A pair of threshold objects (pool entry pillars) stands 1.0-3.0 m inside the door, with a hanging screen (rippling water veil) between them; the route slips through a 1.9 m gap. The narrow gap forces you to slow your pace and mentally shift from the street to the subterranean pool environment.
- **Heroic Void** The hero (drained pool basin) stands alone in an empty 8 x 7 m area (the deep end); the route circles it and never comes closer than 3.4 m. The expansive distance around the central brass basin creates a sense of isolation that elevates the product to a sculptural centerpiece.
- **Peripheral Discovery** Four product stations (sunken eyewear cases) sit at the outer corners, outside the loop, so you turn away from the hero to reach them. By positioning display cases at the corners, the layout encourages you to turn away from the center to engage in intimate product interaction.
- **Closing the Loop** The pay desk (pool attendant station) closes the loop at the back wall. The placement of the attendant station at the rear anchors your path, providing a definitive conclusion to the circular exploration of the pool.

## The walk
- **I step between the cold concrete pillars and beneath the shimmering veil.** The transition marks my departure from the outside world into a quiet, submerged atmosphere.
- **I gaze into the drained basin where brass floors catch the shifting light.** The textile ceiling above creates a rhythmic dance of water-like shadows across the void.
- **I lean toward the sunken glass cases to examine the collection closely.** Tucked into the corners, these illuminated displays serve as intimate anchors within the vast space.
