# Aesop — The Scented Archive
*Where the library ends and the olfactory journey begins in Jeju*

As you step inside, the silence of the dome envelops you, grounding your senses in the raw textures of lime plaster and volcanic stone. Your journey begins at the black stone basin, where you perform a quiet cleansing ritual before exploring the fragmented archive lining the walls. You navigate the winding path between strata-clay shelves, discovering scents housed like precious manuscripts in an ancient library. The experience concludes at the granite counter, leaving you with a lingering sense of stillness and earthen clarity.

**Keywords:** Olfactory Architecture · Jeju Basalt · Meditative Ritual
**Philosophy:** In the silence of the archives, fragrance becomes the story that remains when the books are closed.

## Design intent
- **Fragmented Archive Perimeter** Shelf walls (Fragmented Archive) wrap the left, right and back of the 12 x 10 m room. These layered clay walls create an immersive, library-like atmosphere that encourages you to linger and explore each scent shelf.
- **Centered Ritual Basin** The centre object (Ritual Scent Basin) is the first stop, 4.2 m in from the door. Placing the basin at the heart of the room forces you to pause and engage in a tactile cleansing ritual immediately upon entry.
- **Circulatory Flow** The route circles it first, then follows the shelves. The intentional loop guides your movement, ensuring you experience the sensory transition from the central ritual to the peripheral archive.
- **Granite Counter Placement** The counter (The Stone Counter) sits at the back, off the loop. By tucking the counter away from the primary loop, the space maintains a serene, gallery-like focus on the product rather than the transaction.

## The walk
- **I approach the black stone basin to perform a quiet cleansing ritual.** The cool water grounds my senses, preparing me for the olfactory journey ahead.
- **I trace my fingers across the strata-clay shelves housing the fragrance vials.** Each scent is stored like a precious manuscript within this textured, winding archive.
- **I stand in the center of the dome, looking up at the vast, silent curvature.** The raw lime plaster walls create an atmosphere of total earthen clarity and stillness.
