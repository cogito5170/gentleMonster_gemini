# Aesop — The Courtyard Ritual
*Seochon’s quiet ritual of water and stone.*

As you step into the store, the bustling streets of Seochon fade away, replaced by the profound stillness of an open-air Hanok courtyard. Your journey begins at a central black stone basin, an invitation to a meditative cleansing ritual. You then drift towards the warm, earthy strata-clay walls, where Aesop products are nestled like hidden treasures. The journey concludes at a serene wooden counter at the rear of the space.

**Keywords:** seochon · hanok atmosphere · ritualistic cleansing
**Philosophy:** The pause before the care.

## Design intent
- **Earth-bound architecture** Shelf walls (shelving embedded in stone walls) wrap the left, right and back of the 12 x 10 m room. These grounded, earth-toned shelves anchor the space in the aesthetic of traditional Korean architecture.
- **The ritual of stillness** The centre object (meditative stone basin ritual) is the first stop, 4.2 m in from the door. This central basin forces a purposeful pause, centering the visitor in the present moment through the element of water.
- **Guided by intuition** The route circles it first, then follows the shelves. By drawing the visitor around the central basin, the design ensures the experience is contemplative and flow-driven.
- **Tactile warmth at completion** The counter (minimalist payment counter) sits at the back, off the loop. The wooden counter provides a warm, tactile conclusion to the ritualistic experience.
