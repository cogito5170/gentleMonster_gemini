# Acne Studios — Acne Studios: Site Under Construction
*Acne Studios, Seongsu.*

You enter a space where construction is not an interruption, but a permanent state of being. The raw concrete skeleton of the Seongsu building wraps around you, while the centerpiece—a towering construction crane hook—dangles a single, pristine garment. You navigate through a field of scaffolding pipe displays, where waterproof tarps transform into luxurious garment racks. Here, the boundary between an unfinished site and a curated boutique dissolves into a singular, industrial experience.

**Keywords:** deconstruction · industrial permanence · raw structuralism
**Philosophy:** Fashion is not the final layer, but the skeleton of the space itself.

## Design intent
- **A Towering Anchor** A floor strip (Graphite + wax floor) runs 4.7 m from the door toward the hero (towering construction crane hook). The central tower acts as a gravitational anchor, demanding immediate engagement from the visitor.
- **Visceral Openness** The hero stands alone 5.6 m inside the door, 3.5 m tall; nothing else rises above 1.8 m. The open field ensures a visceral, unobstructed connection to the structural bones of the building.
- **Tactile Scaffolding Displays** Four product stations (scaffolding pipe garment display) ring it loosely, 0.4 m from the route at the closest. The scaffolding stations invite you to touch and interact with the clothing as if selecting tools from a site.
- **Material Grounding** The pay counter (weathered waterproof tarp counter) sits at the back right, where the route ends. The tarp-draped counter grounds the experience in the materiality of the construction site.
