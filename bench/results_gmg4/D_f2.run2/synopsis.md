# Gentle Monster — The Harbor Warehouse Pop-up
*An industrial sanctuary for the restless voyager.*

Step through the threshold of rusted chains into a thick, swirling mist. Your path takes you around the glowing amber lighthouse lens, its rhythmic light casting long, dancing shadows across the warehouse floor. As you explore the peripheral stations, the cold steel and rough textures tell a story of oceanic endurance. Finally, you reach the logbook desk at the back, leaving the fog behind with your chosen piece.

**Keywords:** Maritime Industrial · Lighthouse Pulse · Fogbound Discovery
**Philosophy:** In the stillness of the harbor, only the light remembers the way.

## Design intent
- **Entering the Industrial Fog** A pair of threshold objects (weathered mooring ropes) stands 1.0-3.0 m inside the door, with a hanging screen (rusted chain dividers) between them; the route slips through a 1.9 m gap. You begin your descent into the harbor by navigating through the heavy, hanging chains.
- **The Luminous Anchor** The hero (antique lighthouse lens) stands alone in an empty 8 x 7 m area (Central void); the route circles it and never comes closer than 3.3 m. The lens guides your movement, pulling you into a slow, orbital journey of discovery.
- **Exploring Peripheral Depths** Four product stations (bag display modules) sit at the outer corners, outside the loop, so you turn away from the hero to reach them. Peripheral modules allow you to engage with the collection away from the central glow.
- **Final Port of Call** The pay desk (logbook desk) closes the loop at the back wall. Your journey concludes at the desk where time seems to slow down and anchor.

## The walk
- **I stand between the heavy ropes, feeling the weight of the harbor air.** The transition from city to sea begins here.
- **The amber light pulses against the fog, pulling me closer to the center.** A rhythmic beacon in the heart of the void.
- **I turn to the dark wooden modules, finding beauty in the shadows.** The bags catch the light, distant from the main pulse.
