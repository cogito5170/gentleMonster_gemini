# Acne Studios — The Unfinished Narrative
*Acne Studios Seongsu: A temporal suspension of industrial progress.*

You enter a raw, exposed concrete skeleton in the heart of Seongsu. A towering structure of rusted scaffolding dominates the center, acting as a beacon of industrial progress. Around it, translucent tarp displays float like ghosts, holding the latest collections in a fragile embrace. As you navigate the path to the back-right counter, the boundaries between building site and luxury boutique dissolve.

**Keywords:** Industrial Ruin · Suspended Fashion · Construction Aesthetics
**Philosophy:** Fashion emerges from the chaos of construction.

## Design intent
- **Raw Concrete Pathway** A floor strip (Exposed Structure Pathway) runs 4.7 m from the door toward the hero (Industrial Scaffolding Tower). The concrete path creates a tactile tension, echoing the raw street atmosphere of the neighbourhood.
- **Monolithic Scaffolding Hero** The hero stands alone 5.6 m inside the door, 3.5 m tall; nothing else rises above 1.8 m. The towering scaffolding commands the space, serving as an architectural skeleton for the brand.
- **Ethereal Tarp Displays** Four product stations (Translucent Tarp Displays) ring it loosely, 0.4 m from the route at the closest. These tarp structures soften the industrial environment, offering a surreal display for the collection.
- **Polished Concrete Anchor** The pay counter (Polished Concrete Counter) sits at the back right, where the route ends. The minimalist counter provides a grounding point, reflecting the brand's disciplined focus amidst the surrounding chaos.
