# Gentle Monster — The Dry Threshold
*Where the rain is switched off*

You come in out of the rain and the sound stops at a curtain of still steel cables, hung like rain that forgot to fall. Behind it the floor is wet black stone and the only movement is a round black pool, its surface rippling under a low luminous ceiling. Four dark plinths stand at the edge of the light, each holding a single pair of frames like something left on a windowsill. You circle the pool slowly, dry for the first time tonight, and the frames are the last thing you collect before the desk.

**Keywords:** Threshold · Held rain · Silence
**Philosophy:** The city keeps raining. Inside, only the light is wet.

## Design intent
- **Rain is a wall you pass through** Two cable curtains hang 3.4 m inside the door with a 0.8 m dry gap between them; the street sound ends there.
- **The pool is the only motion** A 2 m black basin sits alone under the luminous ceiling in a 10 x 7 m stone field; the route circles it and never touches it.
- **Frames wait at the edge of the light** Four plinths sit outside the loop at the field's corners, so you step out of the light to try a pair.
- **The desk closes the walk** The pay desk is the far wall; the loop ends facing it, with the rain behind you.

## The walk
- **The rain stops at the stone.** Steel cables hang still like held rain. I slip through the dry gap.
- **Only the pool is still moving.** A black circle ripples under a ceiling of soft light.
- **I step out of the light to choose.** One pair of frames waits on the plinth, dry.
